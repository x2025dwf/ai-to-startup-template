            About PlanPal

            PlanPal is a website where students enter their class schedule and assignment rubrics, and it creates a daily plan and shows when everything is due.

            A student project prototype

            This demo stores the classes, assignments, demo sign-in state, and selected sample plan only in this browser on this device. It does not use a real AI, account, server, or payment processor.

            The study plan uses your estimated work time and due dates to suggest short focus sessions for assignments coming up in the next week. It is a planning aid, not a guarantee that work will be finished on time.

            Student prototype for BSAD 281 at St. Francis Xavier University. Not a real company — all data is fictional.
